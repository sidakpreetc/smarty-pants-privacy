# Privacy Policy for Swigly Words

**Last updated:** July 31, 2026

Swigly Words ("the App") is developed and published by Smarty Pants ("we," "us," "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

## Information We Collect

**Account Information (optional)**
If you choose to create an account instead of playing as a guest, we collect your email address and any profile information you provide (such as a display name), via our backend provider, Supabase.

**Gameplay Data**
We store your game progress, including levels completed, stars earned, hints used, and related activity. If you play as a guest, this data is stored locally on your device only. If you sign in, this data is stored in our backend (Supabase) and associated with your account.

**Purchase Information**
If you purchase hint packs, the transaction is processed by Google Play (or Apple, where applicable) and our subscription/purchase management provider, RevenueCat. We receive confirmation of your purchase and the product purchased, but we do not receive or store your payment card details.

**Friends and Social Features**
If you use friend-related features (where available), we store the connections between accounts (friend requests, accepted friendships) and any hint-related activity shared between friends you've connected with.

## How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Save and sync your game progress
- Enable account sign-in and guest-to-account migration
- Process purchases and grant purchased content
- Enable friends and social features (where available)
- Maintain and improve the App

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party service providers, who may process data on our behalf:
- **Supabase** — backend database, authentication
- **RevenueCat** — purchase/subscription management
- **Google Play** (and Apple App Store, where applicable) — app distribution and payment processing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## Advertising

The App does not currently display advertisements. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated to describe any advertising partners and data used for that purpose before such a feature is enabled.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Guest data is stored only on your device and can be cleared by uninstalling the App or clearing app data. If you have a registered account, you may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at support.smartypantscreations@gmail.com.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
